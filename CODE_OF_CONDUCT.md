# Code of Conduct

Lisa welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds and experience.
Participation in project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
maintainer-managed spaces requires respectful, constructive behavior.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical decisions and evidence without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept good-faith questions.
- Respect privacy, consent, and other people's time.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and correct mistakes honestly.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention,
deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information
are not acceptable.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, limit participation,
or exclude participants when necessary to protect the community. Responses
should consider severity, context, repeated behavior, and the safety of
affected people. Disagreement with a technical decision is not misconduct.

## Reporting

**Confidential conduct contact is not yet configured.** Maintainers must publish
and test a private reporting channel before public release. Do not publish
sensitive incident details or identify affected people in a public issue.
If necessary, request a private maintainer contact without disclosing details.

Report non-sensitive moderation problems to repository maintainers through
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/Lisa-Harness/lisa/issues). A maintainer
involved in a report should not be its sole reviewer. Keep reports limited
to the information needed to investigate.

Vulnerability reports use the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).
